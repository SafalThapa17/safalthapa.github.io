## About Me
** Quantitative Research | M.S. Data Science & Engineering | Python, Statistics, Probability & Machine Learning | Systematic Research | Agro Enterprenuer **

I work at the intersection of data, economics and agriculture. 

Before graduate school, I co-founded and ran a post-harvest storage business in Nepal for four years, storing vegetable seeds and other commodities for farmers and traders. That experience showed me how much of a farmer’s income depends on decisions made after harvest: when to sell, whether to store, and how to manage spoilage and price risk. It also showed me how little reliable data exists to guide those decisions.

I hold a B.S. in Computer Science from Webster University and an M.S. in Data Science and Engineering from The City College of New York. I use these skills as tools to study economic and social questions.
