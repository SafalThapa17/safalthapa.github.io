My teaching journey started when I was 16, volunteering to teach English to first-grade monks at Pema T’sal Buddhist Monastery in Pokhara for 3 months. Those classes helped me realize how much I enjoy teaching, talking with students, and building connections with them. Being in a classroom and interacting with students soon became one of the things I enjoyed most.
