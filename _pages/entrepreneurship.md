## Hemja Agro Private Limited, Co-founder
1000 MT Post-harvest storage · Pokhara, Nepal · 2019 – Present
· [Website](https://www.hemjaagro.com.np/)

I co-founded a post-harvest storage facility in 2019 and ran its day-to-day operations until 2024, when I moved to the United States for graduate school. The facility serves more than 700 farmers each year. Our core business is storing potato seed, where reliable conditions protect seed quality through the months between harvest and planting.

We also expanded to serve the wider local food economy. Fruit vendors store apples and cheese with us to keep them fresh longer, and nearby poultry farmers use our facility for safe, hygienic meat storage. By protecting quality and extending shelf life, we help farmers and vendors store and sell their products with confidence.

**What we do**
- Stored potato seed for 700+ farmers a year, protecting quality until planting season
- Provided cold storage for apples, cheese and poultry meat for local vendors and poultry farmers
- Let farmers and traders hold produce instead of selling at harvest-time prices

**What I learned**
- Much of a farmer's income depends on decisions made after harvest: when to sell, whether to store, and how to manage spoilage and price risk.
- Reliable data to guide those decisions rarely exists.

**From Business to Research**
Running the facility left me with questions that I could not answer from the business side alone. When farmers store their harvest, are they actually able to sell at better prices, or do storage costs and price changes reduce those gains? Who benefits most from storage: larger farmers, smallholders, or traders? And what makes it difficult for small storage businesses like mine to grow? Is it access to credit, uncertain demand, or a lack of market information to make better decisions?

These questions have made me curious about the role of data and research in understanding post-harvest storage, price risk, and farmer welfare. I want to explore these questions through careful data analysis and evidence, and understand how research can help farmers, businesses, and policymakers make better decisions about where storage can have the greatest impact. This is also what has led me to explore PhD research in this area.
